# Appendix B: Gate Stack Dielectric Properties and Thermodynamic Data

## Physical Properties of Key Materials

### Silicon (Si)

| Property | Value | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density (300K) | 2.33 | g/cm³ | Single crystal |
| Melting Point | 1414 | °C | Absolute limit for process temperature |
| Thermal Conductivity (300K) | 149 | W/m·K | Decreases with temperature |
| Thermal Expansion | 2.6 | ppm/K | Low CTE, good with SiO₂ |
| Specific Heat | 712 | J/kg·K | Moderate heat capacity |

### Silicon Dioxide (SiO₂)

| Property | Value | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density (300K) | 2.20-2.27 | g/cm³ | Varies with deposition method |
| Melting Point | 1710 | °C | Very high thermal stability |
| Thermal Conductivity (300K) | 1.4 | W/m·K | Very poor conductor; insulating |
| Thermal Expansion | 0.5 | ppm/K | Very low; good for stress matching |
| Specific Heat | 740 | J/kg·K | High per unit mass |
| Refractive Index (633nm) | 1.46 | — | Transparent in visible |

### Silicon Nitride (Si₃N₄)

| Property | Value | Unit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Density (300K) | 3.18-3.24 | g/cm³ | Denser than SiO₂ |
| Melting Point | 1900 | °C | Extremely high; very stable |
| Thermal Conductivity (300K) | 30-33 | W/m·K | Better than oxide; worse than Si |
| Thermal Expansion | 3.3 | ppm/K | Slightly higher than Si |
| Specific Heat | 750 | J/kg·K | Similar to oxide |
| Refractive Index (633nm) | 1.9-2.0 | — | Higher than oxide |
| Dielectric Constant | 7-8 | — | Higher than SiO₂ (~3.9) |
